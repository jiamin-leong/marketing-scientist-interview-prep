# Marketing Scientist Interview Prep — Snap

Interactive cheatsheet, product glossary, Mandarin phrase list, and flashcards for the
Marketing Science Partner (China performance advertisers) interview at Snap Inc.

Covers experimentation & incrementality (holdout vs. add-on tests, ghost ads, budget
parity), driver analysis (product-side vs. auction-side, regression diagnostics), case
study structure, Snap's ad/measurement product vocabulary, and English↔Mandarin phrasing
for client-facing terms and % gains.

Open the live site or `index.html` locally. Say each flashcard answer out loud, then
reveal. Filter by cluster, shuffle, and star cards for review.
